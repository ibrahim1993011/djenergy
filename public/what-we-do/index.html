WHAT WE DO-DJENERGY.

Welcome to DJENERGY, a cells-to-system energy storage manufacturer. We design and supply LiFePO4 battery cells, modules/packs, high-voltage rack batteries, and complete energy storage systems for commercial & industrial (C&I), microgrid, and home backup applications. Our focus is to deliver safe, reliable, and project-ready solutions that help customers reduce energy costs, improve power reliability, and achieve measurable ROI.

Vision: To accelerate the global energy transition with safe, scalable LiFePO4 energy storage—built from cells to complete systems.
Mission: To provide bankable energy storage products and integrated solutions (ESS + EMS + protection) that are easy to deploy, easy to maintain, and engineered for long service life across different grid conditions and hybrid power sources.

Core Values

Safety First – multi-layer protection, strict testing, and quality control.
Engineering Integrity – transparent specifications, real project design, and consistent performance.
Customer Success – fast response, reliable delivery, and lifecycle support.

Our Products

LiFePO4 Battery Cells High-consistency prismatic cells engineered for long cycle life and stable performance. Cell selection and matching available for project requirements.

Battery Modules & Packs (OEM/ODM) Customizable voltage/capacity designs with communication options, optimized for manufacturability, serviceability, and thermal safety.

High-Voltage (HV) Rack Battery Systems Scalable HV racks for C&I ESS and large power applications, designed for stable DC performance and mainstream PCS/EMS integration.

All-in-One C&I Energy Storage System (Air-Cooled) Integrated cabinet ESS combining battery + PCS + EMS-ready control and protection for peak shaving, TOU shifting, backup power, and PV self-consumption.

Containerized BESS (Air/Liquid Cooling) Scalable container solutions for larger capacities and demanding sites, supporting grid-connected, off-grid, and hybrid operation.

Backup Power for Critical Loads Fast response backup with optional STS/ATS integration to keep critical loads running during grid disturbances.

Home Backup Energy Storage Safe LiFePO4 home batteries for backup and self-consumption, designed for easy installation and stable daily cycling.

Solar energy harnesses the power of the sun to provide clean

renewable, and sustainable electricity. This eco-friendly solution reduces dependence on fossil fuels and decreases greenhouse gas emissions, making it an essential component of the global shift towards greener energy sources

solar system consists of solar panels, inverters, batteries

and other components that work together to convert sunlight into usable electricity. Ideal for both residential and commercial use, solar systems offer a reliable and cost-effective way to generate power, significantly lowering energy bills and increasing energy independence

LiFePO4 batteries, or lithium iron phosphate batteries

are a type of lithium-ion battery known for their strong safety profile, long lifespan, and stability under extreme conditions. They are a popular choice in solar energy systems due to their efficiency in energy storage, helping to ensure a steady supply of power even when the sun isn't shining.
