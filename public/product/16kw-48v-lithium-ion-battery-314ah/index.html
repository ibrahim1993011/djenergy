 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use this battery with my existing inverter?
Yes, as long as it supports 48V lithium batteries. Most hybrid and off-grid inverters are compatible.

Q: Is it safe to install indoors?
Absolutely. LiFePO₄ is the safest lithium chemistry and emits no gases or harmful fumes.

Q: Can I charge this with solar panels?
Yes. Just pair it with a compatible charge controller or hybrid inverter for solar integration.

Q: How long does the battery last?
Typically 10–15 years or more depending on usage and depth of discharge.
