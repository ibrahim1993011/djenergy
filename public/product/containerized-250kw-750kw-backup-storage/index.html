Description

Containerized BESS with 250kW PCS and 750kWh Battery: Modular Backup Storage for Grid-Connected Power Systems

As energy grids become smarter, cleaner, and more decentralized, the demand for flexible energy storage continues to surge. A containerized Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) featuring a 250kW PCS and 750kWh of battery capacity offers a high-performance solution for both commercial and industrial applications. Whether you’re looking to support the power grid, provide backup storage, or optimize energy usage from renewables, this integrated unit delivers.

Designed for rapid deployment, grid compatibility, and seamless scalability, this containerized BESS provides up to 3 hours of continuous power at full output—perfect for peak shaving, emergency backup, or renewable smoothing.

What is a Containerized BESS with 250kW PCS and 750kWh Storage?

This system packages a 250kW Power Conversion System (PCS) with 750kWh of lithium-ion battery storage into a robust, portable container. The PCS controls the flow of electricity in and out of the batteries, managing charging, discharging, and interfacing with the power grid. The batteries store excess electricity and release it on demand—whether to reduce grid strain or to act as backup storage during outages.

The container includes:

  • 250kW bidirectional PCS
  • 750kWh of modular battery packs
  • Battery Management System (BMS)
  • Energy Management System (EMS)
  • Fire suppression and HVAC
  • Integrated safety and remote monitoring

This configuration is engineered for plug-and-play integration, minimizing installation time and maximizing reliability.
